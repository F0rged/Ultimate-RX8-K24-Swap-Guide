# 05 — Dry-fit, firewall and front-structure work

This is the most fabrication-intensive section of the swap.

## 5.1 Prepare the engine for *mock-up*, not final assembly

For the first dry-fit, install only the components that define hard clearance:

- correct oil pan/pump or a dimensionally equivalent mock-up
- final low-profile/intake-side coolant neck
- valve cover
- cam/crank sensors that protrude toward the firewall
- final engine mount brackets
- adapter plate
- transmission
- starter if its envelope affects bellhousing
- crank pulley
- steering rack in its intended position

Leave intake, header and most accessories off unless they define a conflict.

## 5.2 Fix the drivetrain datum first

On the retained-RX-8-transmission path, the **transmission/PPF relationship is the datum**. Do not move the transmission to make the engine fit and then discover that the PPF/driveshaft no longer align.

Sequence:

1. mate K24, adapter and RX-8 transmission per the adapter manufacturer's instructions;
2. remove shifter if required for installation;
3. support the differential/PPF;
4. place the engine/transmission into the bay;
5. bring the transmission into its intended PPF position without forcing it;
6. only then judge firewall, rack and oil-pan conflicts.

Independent Collins-path build logs report that the PPF's elongated mounting holes provided enough adjustment for the slightly shifted transmission position. Verify this on the actual S2 rather than relying on that anecdote.

## 5.3 Firewall/cowl process

### Do not start with a copied cut dimension

Current evidence is configuration-dependent:

- Collins instructs approximately 1.5 inches of firewall recess in the center for its setup and says cutting through is not required.
- Another completed K24 RX-8 documented roughly a two-inch recess/cowl surgery.
- A JDM K24A/RBB build ultimately cut and resealed the firewall to clear the rear of the K24 head/coolant outlet.
- KPower says its RX-8-compatible architecture should be expected to require extensive firewall cutting/welding.

### Prescriptive method

1. Remove the engine-bay heat shield in the affected area.
2. Pull back interior insulation and identify HVAC plastic, wiring and flammable material behind the work area.
3. Install the engine/trans at the fixed driveline datum.
4. Mark every hard interference: head casting, coolant neck, cam sensors, valve cover, breather and service access.
5. Add an **engine-movement and service margin**, not merely static contact clearance.
6. Recess the firewall where practical.
7. If required, cut the minimum useful section to create a serviceable pocket.
8. Form and weld a proper replacement panel.
9. Seal seams, prime/paint bare steel and apply suitable heat shielding.
10. Reinstall the engine and verify that the valve cover and critical sensors can still be serviced.

Do not hammer against hidden HVAC plastic, wiring or brake components.

## 5.4 Crossmember / subframe — Collins path

Current Collins instructions require the factory front crossmember to be notched for oil-pan clearance.

Procedure:

1. Keep the rack/subframe assembly controlled so it cannot distort while unloaded.
2. Mark the minimum notch from repeated mock-up.
3. Preserve suspension pickup points.
4. Cut only after the final pan and rack position are known.
5. Box/reinforce the opening using sound structural fabrication practice.
6. Check the finished structure for:
   - oil-pan clearance under engine movement
   - rack clearance
   - sway-bar clearance
   - drain-plug/service access
   - no weld distortion at chassis pickup points.

If you are not qualified to determine how the notch should be reinforced, have a motorsport/chassis fabrication shop do this work.

## 5.5 ABS hydraulic-unit relocation

Both Collins instructions and independent builds identify ABS relocation as a likely requirement.

Preferred process:

- first try to relocate the complete unit using existing line flexibility without kinking;
- keep hard lines away from header heat and engine movement;
- make a rigid bracket;
- preserve connector strain relief;
- if lines must be remade, use proper brake hard line, flare standard and tools;
- pressure-test and bleed the system fully.

**Do not** delete/bypass ABS casually on a street car. If ABS/DSC functionality cannot be validated electronically after the swap, treat that as a deliberate safety-system decision, not a way to extinguish a warning lamp.

## 5.6 Starter/bellhousing clearance — Collins path

Collins' current instructions call for a small relief in the RX-8 bellhousing so the K-series starter clears. Follow the current vendor instructions for the exact adapter revision.

Do not enlarge the relief beyond what is required and inspect for adequate material around nearby slave-cylinder and bellhousing features.

## Fabrication Gate G3

Pass when:

- engine/trans sits at the intended driveline datum without bolt-force alignment
- firewall/cowl has dynamic and service clearance
- crossmember/subframe repair is complete
- steering clears crank pulley and oil pan
- ABS unit and lines are secure and heat-safe
- starter, coolant neck, sensors and valve cover are serviceable
- no bare/unsealed firewall or structural metal remains
