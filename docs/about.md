# About this site

This site is a research-backed, community-maintained guide for planning Honda K-series swaps into Mazda RX-8 chassis.

It distinguishes documented, partially documented and custom/unvalidated combinations. Year, market, steering-side, transmission, engine and electronics nuances live in the chapters where they change parts or procedure; no universal fitment claim is implied.

It is not a factory service manual or a guarantee that any specific parts combination will fit your car. Treat it as a structured starting point: verify measurements, vendor notes, wiring details and safety-critical decisions against your own chassis before cutting, welding, ordering parts or starting the car.
